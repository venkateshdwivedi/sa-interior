  type, hairline rules), borrowed for "What we make".

## 2. What this site feels like (one sentence, no generic adjective)

shadow onto an ivory wall, every sample within arm's reach.

## 4. Headline + signature move
- **Headline:** "We fit out the whole room." / *em:* "Down to the jaali-fine edge."
  (Differs from every sibling headline; concrete; ties to the motif.)
- **Signature move (the thing a visitor describes):** the **jaali** — section edges are
  cut like a carved Lucknow lattice screen, drawn in a thin haldi-gold line, so each
  section rises into the one above it through a pierced lattice band instead of a plain
  seam. Replaces the old chevron-seam motif.
- **One animated moment stays the 3D KITCHEN build** (unchanged mechanism): the kitchen
  goes into an empty room on scroll, steps advance, the rail fills — nothing else moves.
